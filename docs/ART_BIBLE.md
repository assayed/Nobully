# HeroKid art bible

1. **Style**: flat cartoon storybook. Rounded shapes, no outlines, big heads (≈⅓ of the body), Egyptian everyday settings (school with the flag, Cairo apartment blocks, metro, club pool, home living room).
2. **Palette** (source: `src/theme.ts`, scene colours in `src/art/`): background `#FFF7E8` warm cream · primary `#FF7A00` hero orange · good `#16A34A` · wrong `#E11D48` · coin `#FACC15` · purple `#7C3AED` (daily, badges) · sky `#38BDF8`. Skin tones `#F7D7BC #EBB98F #C98E62 #8E5B3C`.
3. **Mood**: bright, sunny daytime; feedback is never scary. Wrong answers show sadness, never punishment. Good answers celebrate with cheering, confetti and coins.

Rules: every tap gets a sound and a squish within 0.1 s; numbers count up, never snap; characters always gently breathe; the child's own hero appears in every situation.
